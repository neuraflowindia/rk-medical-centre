## Home opening: a clear visual introduction

Keep the current headline, **Doctor consultations in Lucknow.** Replace the long list in the paragraph with one short line: **Choose the care you need and book a visit.** Put the specialties in the artwork, using real selectable labels rather than words generated inside a picture.

On a computer, retain text on the left and the large stethoscope composition on the right. Four compact specialty groups settle around the instrument in reserved positions; nothing covers the headline or buttons. On a phone, the four small picture-and-label choices appear before the large decorative instrument, so people learn the specialties without scrolling through a large picture first.

The four labels are quick routes into the two care areas, not four different departments. Physician care and General health lead to different topics within the same physician chapter. The women's label includes the formal specialty and its ordinary-language explanation.

Remove the visible “Illustration” captions. Ordinary images can have accurate, short descriptions such as “Stethoscope” without a warning label. The private comparison interface will carry review-only information; the intended website layout will not be covered with “sample” or “made-up” labels. This does not authorize invented facts or presenting an unconnected form as a successful booking.

## Artwork: one family, several materials

The cream-background cutout style is best described as **layered paper-cut illustration** or **paper-relief illustration**. It looks like shapes cut from thick paper, arranged in shallow layers and photographed under soft light. “Editorial paper sculpture” is another useful phrase. It is not simply a flat icon or generic 3D cartoon.

Reusable prompt:

> Create a layered paper-cut editorial illustration of [SUBJECT]. Use sculpted layers of matte ivory, muted teal, navy and pale-blue paper on a warm cream background. Show gently raised edges, fine paper texture, shallow relief and soft shadows from a large light source at the upper left. Use a simple, clearly recognizable silhouette and a few carefully placed supporting shapes. Keep the composition spacious and refined. No text, letters, numbers, logos, room interior, realistic patient or doctor. Keep the whole subject visible with generous clear edges. Landscape composition suitable for a healthcare website.

For realistic objects, use **studio still-life product photography of an unbranded medical instrument**, with the same colours, lighting and background. Use paper images for human themes, realistic generic objects for instruments, and precisely drawn lines/shapes for movement. Avoid mixing unrelated painting/cartoon/neon styles on the same page.

Keep all six current images. Plan four new generated masters: a generic glucose meter with a blank display; thermometer/case; blank report folder with pen; and a layered adult-profile care composition. Generate separate objects or transparent cutouts where independent motion is required. A flattened image cannot make its internal parts assemble separately.

## Movement that people can actually see

Use a clear order: **background pieces arrive → main object settles → supporting pieces join → fine lines finish**. Headings and booking buttons remain usable throughout.

- Main Home composition: about 2.2 seconds on a computer, 1.5 seconds on a phone.
- Each Treatments chapter: about 1.8 seconds, shortened to 1.2 seconds on a phone.
- About / doctor compositions: 1.2–1.6 seconds.
- Visit steps: 1.8 seconds, now involving each full row.
- Manual changes and buttons: 120–240 milliseconds.
- Loops: a few separate decorative pieces drift 4–8 pixels over 8–12 seconds; words, controls and medical readings remain still.

Show one main repeating artwork movement in the visible area at a time. Pause it offscreen, in a hidden comparison pane, while interacting with its section, and when the visitor chooses reduced movement. Provide one reachable pause control beside the selected artwork, linked to the same preference as the footer and review controls; it stays available after pausing. Turning movement off shows the finished composition. Phones get their own tighter arrangements, not shrunken desktop scenes.

## Visit form and visitor measurement

The owner selected **visit form + optional visit measurement**. Cookies alone do not identify a visitor. Names and contact details must come from the form they choose to complete.

The original site's Home pop-up waits seven seconds. Its submission only displays an alert; it sends no request. Its cookie banner stores the choice but is not connected to visit measurement. We should restore the useful interface, then connect it to the agreed real booking route when operational implementation is authorized.

The owner selected continuation into appointment booking. The Home form and pop-up therefore lead into the same appointment process, carrying details forward. There is no separate callback-request journey. Continuing to booking does not mean the appointment is already confirmed.

Use equal, understandable choices for optional measurement: **Allow visit measurement** / **Continue without it**. The booking form works either way. Store the preference, provide a way to change it, and stop optional measurement after withdrawal. Do not send names, phone numbers, email, medical topics, form contents or appointment details to the measurement service. The next local comparison can demonstrate these states without collecting or transmitting real data.
