# Project 001 — six-image production brief for the proposed study

13 October 2026. Production complete: six generated masters and eighteen measured WebP renditions. See asset provenance (local workspace reference: `03-client-projects/001-doctors/0003-generated-assets/00000-medical-illustrations/README.md`) and the manifest. This document supports [the comparison proposal](11-comparison-and-richer-visual-plan.md).

## Shared art direction

Use a related set of four object pictures and two clearly drawn paper illustrations, with the same soft studio light, quiet shadows, navy/teal details and warm-white/pale-blue surfaces. Mint and peach are small accents. The paper pictures must look like paper artwork, never photographs of patients. No clinic interiors, exteriors, real-looking people, identifiable faces, hands, logos, personal data, diagnostic results, approval marks, watermarks or words baked into the pixels. Instruments are illustrative, not claimed clinic possessions.

The image generator makes the object pictures and paper artwork. Native graphics supply accurate labels, category symbols, lines and interactive markers. Do not ask the image model to render medical charts, explanatory text, calendars with operational availability or anatomy that would require clinical verification.

Built-in image generation is the intended route. Generate each distinct asset with its own prompt; no CLI/API provider or new key. For the hero set `transparent_background:true`; the other five use opaque backgrounds. Inspect every output before use. Preserve masters outside deployable source, under `0003-generated-assets/00000-medical-illustrations/masters/`; optimized review copies live beside a manifest in `.../web/`. Copy only selected files into a future application after that work is approved. These folders/assets now exist and are used by the isolated local study; nothing has been copied into the original application.

Create G01 and G02 first, inspect their colours/light/materials together, then use that selected pair as style guidance for G03–G06. Keep the six subjects different while matching the light and softness. A style reference must not make the model copy the wrong object into another care picture. Review internally and correct within the agreed build; do not create a new owner approval step for each image.

## Prompt set

G01 — Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated website hero object. Create a high-quality editorial photograph-like illustration of one generic acoustic stethoscope, petrol-blue flexible tubing and brushed silver chestpiece, softly curved as a complete recognizable object. Physically coherent Y connection, two earpieces, one chestpiece, no impossible joins or duplicated tubing. Three-quarter overhead view, soft directional light, fine material texture. Fully transparent background, generous clean space around the complete object. No person, hand, room, furniture, brand, packaging, text, waveform or display. Request a square high-resolution composition around 2048px. The subject is illustrative healthcare imagery, not an actual clinic's equipment.

G02 — Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: women's-health care illustration. Create a carefully made matte-paper artwork of an adult female side-profile silhouette, using a few clean overlapping warm-white, pale-blue and restrained teal paper shapes. It must be plainly recognizable as flat paper-cut artwork, without realistic facial features, skin texture or an identifiable person. Photograph the paper construction with soft studio daylight and shallow natural paper shadows on a warm-white surface. No fashion styling, pink stereotype, stethoscope, medical anatomy, treatment scene or decorative words. Wide 3:2 composition; defining shape inside the central 60%, generous edges for square crops. No clinic, real patient, doctor, hands, logo or watermark.
G03 — Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: ultrasound service still life. Create an original generic external ultrasound transducer with a single physically coherent cable resting in a gentle curve, close-up on a softly lit powder-blue and mint studio surface. Emphasize rounded probe material and cable detail; no branding or specific proprietary model. Wide 3:2 composition, whole defining probe visible in the central 60%, generous edges. No monitor, screen, fetus image, diagnostic scan, patient, body, hand, room, gel procedure, words or watermark. Photo-like editorial quality; instrument illustration only.

G04 — Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pregnancy-care illustration. Create a simple side-profile paper-cut illustration of a pregnant adult figure, with a gentle curved outline and no face or realistic human detail. Clearly handmade matte-paper artwork in the same visual family as the women's-health illustration: warm-white and pale-blue layers, a small restrained peach accent, soft natural studio light and visible paper edges. No baby, baby products, skin texture, clinical anatomy, treatment scene, promised outcome or words. Wide 3:2; the complete defining form stays inside the central 60%, with generous edges for square crops. No clinic room, real patient, doctor, hands, logo or watermark.
G05 — Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: physician-care service still life. Create an original editorial close-up of a generic manual blood-pressure cuff with a physically coherent tube and small mechanical gauge with its face turned away, on a pale-blue surface with a restrained navy accent and a little mint. Convincing fabric, rubber and metal; avoid glossy futuristic design. Wide 3:2, entire defining cuff/bulb/gauge group within the central 60%. Show coherent tube/valve connections. Do not invent or blur gauge markings; its turned-away face avoids any measurement claim. No digital screen, diagnosis, ECG, patient, hand, room, brand, instruction text or watermark. This is generic illustrative healthcare equipment.

G06 — Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: About/visit-process supporting still life. Create an original editorial photograph-like arrangement of a closed plain cream notebook and one simple navy pen on a clean pale-blue surface, with warm natural light and gentle shadows. Wide 3:2 composition, defining objects inside the central 60% with generous margins for responsive crops. No appointment details, dates, handwriting, chart, person, hands, room, brand or watermark. Tactile and calm; suggest preparing questions before a visit without showing real personal information.

## Inspection, iteration and delivery

Check complete silhouettes, instrument construction, transparency/edge halos, convincing materials, colour consistency, absence of text/logos/results and phone crop legibility. G02/G04 must look deliberately illustrated, never like invented patient photographs. Reject cute cartoon styling, glossy toy figures or a paper shape that becomes meaningless at phone size. Do not add extra props simply to make two pictures different. A malformed medical object is rejected, not hidden with blur. Iterate the failed property only; retain approved palette/composition. Do not replace an unacceptable output with an unrelated room or a fake doctor.

Record prompt, tool mode, generation identity/path, actual MIME/dimensions, hash, selected crop/focal point and rendition bytes. Requested size is a brief, not a claim that the tool returned that size. Preserve original outputs non-destructively. Use supported local encoding after inspection; verify MIME/alpha and dimensions, never edit an extension to pretend a different format.

Suggested responsive long-edge widths: hero 480/800/1200; service cards 480/800; larger detail 1200 only when needed. Hero budget ≤300KB desktop and ≤180KB phone; four selected card renditions together ≤600KB; each supporting detail ≤250KB. These are targets requiring measurement, not delivered counts. Prefer sharp enough subjects and measured optimization over destroying texture to hit a number. Document a remaining budget miss before any production approval.

Preload only the visible hero, not six masters or all variants. Give images intrinsic dimensions, appropriate sizes/srcset, async decoding and below-fold lazy loading. If the hero cannot decode promptly, show the complete native care artwork and readable copy; late media can fade in without restarting the whole scene. The original comparison side keeps its original images untouched, including any imagery the owner has now rejected for the proposed side.
