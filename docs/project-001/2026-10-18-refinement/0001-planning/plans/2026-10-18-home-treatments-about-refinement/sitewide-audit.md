## Critique and rejected additions

More content is useful when it answers who the doctors are, what care they provide and how to visit. It is not useful to add another unrequested review carousel, a second booking form, a huge new biography, an extra trust-badge wall or repeated decorative art simply to make every page longer. The existing directory, doctor pages and Contact already contain the relevant detail; the current gaps are concentrated in Home's opening, Treatments' visual hierarchy/art and About's doctor/practical content.

Rejected for this pass: a new color/font system; replacing the stethoscope; enlarging its crop; moving all four hero cards; removing other assembly/loops; new floating construction marks; repeated nested image frames; artificial phone25%height; new procedure/service IDs; expanded medical claims; invented certification dates; fabricated clinic-founding history; new form/analytics destination; reopening provider setup.

Useful later inputs, separate from this plan: actual doctor photographs; personal introductions/achievements in the doctors' own words; clinic history with real dates; confirmed accessibility/arrival information. Their absence does not block this fully populated private layout plan. Add them to their existing logical place when supplied; no new page architecture is needed.

The [strengthened contracts](implementation-contracts.md) make the neutral name/initials portrait treatment the proposed default, so genuine photos can arrive later without blocking the rest. They also close the slow-image/focus, About resize/font, shared treatment-row and narrow-action details without expanding the page map above.

## Research used

Primary references checked18October2026:

- [W3C: headings and labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html): short headings should describe the topic/purpose. Used for direct patient wording and distinct care-column heading areas.
- [W3C: minimum contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): readable foreground/background pairing. Applied to the calculated blue/green bands and visible facts.
- [W3C: animation from interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html): preserve the operating-system reduced-movement alternative while implementing the owner's requested fade.

These references guide presentation and accessibility; they are not evidence of medical capability or clinic credentials. No new medical advice or condition-specific treatment recommendation is introduced.
